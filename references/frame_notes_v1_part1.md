## Rules confirmed by this pass
1. **Something animates inside the shot about every second** (9.5 per 10 s). A held frame never lasts more than about 3.8 s.
2. **Light is animated:** candles flicker, rain falls, windows flicker, searchlights sweep, lamps click on, lens flares pass.
3. **The camera moves between scenes physically** (pulls out through a window, tilts under the table, flies through a light tunnel) as often as it cuts.
4. **Text is always typed** (cards, tags, titles), never faded in whole.
5. **Lists and "everything" are shown item by item** (beams on the map, furniture sold, polaroids).
6. **Fills and highlights:** places fill with colour (Taiwan orange, USA blue); routes draw as dotted arcs.
