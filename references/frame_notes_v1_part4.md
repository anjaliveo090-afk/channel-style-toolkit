## New rules from part 4
1. **Rip/tear transition.** A crack or lightning tear opens the frame onto the next scene. Used twice, for "becomes" moments.
2. **Writing happens live.** A visible marker or hand writes the words as they are said; the board **scrolls** when full.
3. **Numbers grow physically.** A stack or tower rises with the counter; at the peak, a speed-line burst and particles.
4. **Every new prop enters with motion.** It slides in, drops with a bounce, or flies in; nothing just appears, except text cards.
5. **Long sentences on black cards use small, left-aligned text.** Short lines are bigger and centred.
6. **Callback sets are reused exactly** (the dish station) to tie the past to the present.
7. **The "zero" device:** a circled and slashed 0 on a clipboard, an empty plate with crumbs.
8. **Crowds have tiny idle bobs and camera-flash sparkles.**
