# V1 part 1 (0:00-1:17.7): first look notes (to be folded into style_bible / visual_grammar)

**File:** 1280x720, 24 fps, AV1 + AAC 44.1 kHz stereo. Working copy `references/video/v1_part1.h264.mp4`; OpenCV cannot decode AV1.
**Sheets:** `output/ref/v1/p1_*.png` (4 fps).

## Look
- **Rendering:** flat 2D vector cartoon, not painterly. The thumbnails are a separate, richer illustration style.
- **Heads:** big circle, about 0.35-0.45 of character height. Peach/cream skin, thick dark outline (about 3-4% of head width). A soft lit rim-glow arc inside the outline on the light side. No nose. Small curved-line eyebrows.
- **Eyes:** dot eyes, or closed happy arcs when laughing. Big open D-shaped mouth for laughs (white teeth band, pink tongue); a flat dash for deadpan.
- **Hair:** a solid cap shape with a few strand lines (grey for Jensen, black bob for Alice, black buns and caps for others).
- **Glasses:** thin black rectangles. Alice wears black sunglasses.
- **Body:** a small rounded egg/sack torso in clothing colour with a thin outline; collar details (a white V shirt, a jacket lapel).
- **Limbs:** **thin black stick lines** for arms and legs, about 1/10 of the head outline. Tiny stick fingers (3-4 lines) holding props. Feet are tiny line ends.
- **Proportions:** in wide shots characters are about 2.3-2.6 heads tall (head plus a short torso and stick legs).
- **Lighting:** warm key light, cinematic.
  - Candle glow, bloom and soft bokeh at the dinner.
  - Practical light sources: wall sconces, lamps, window glow.
  - Strong vignette; night scenes in deep navy.
  - Depth of field: the background is blurred in close-ups.
- **Sets:** flat vector interiors (living room with sofa, lamp, TV, bicycle, fridge; porch with a swing at sunset; hallway), a world map with clouds (paper-map palette: sand land, light-blue sea), a city skyline at night, a desk with a rotary phone, a corkboard with photos and red string, a top-down table plan.
- **Overlays and graphics:**
  - Lower-third **name tags**: a dark box, a green accent bar, white handwritten caps "JENSEN HUANG".
  - Camera viewfinder frame with a crosshair and REC (surveillance).
  - Polaroid photos dropping in.
  - Handwritten yellow-green label "$ BILLIONS" over a glowing chip.
  - "AGE 9" handwritten label; tags on suitcases ("TPE").
  - Stamps ("SECRET", red "CHINA" with a star).
- **Text cards:** a **pure black screen with white handwritten script text typed on letter by letter**, top-left or centred.
  - Used for key lines: "having dinner with a Chinese spy.", "Jensen has no idea.", "The kind China isn't allowed to have.", "He started in a bathroom in Kentucky.", "It was a reform school for troubled kids,".
  - About 1 card per 10-15 s, each lasting about 1.5-2.5 s.
- **Title and chapter cards:**
  - The video title is typed onto the desk scene ("HOW A CHINESE SPY GOT INSIDE NVIDIA") with a red redaction bar (0:40-0:41).
  - The chapter title is centred white script on black ("The Man With the Key", about 2.2 s, 0:41.75-0:44).

## Motion and pace (eyeballed; to be measured with new_visuals.py / flow.py)
- A new visual every about 1-2.5 s.
- Transitions:
  - **Whip-pan with heavy motion blur** (2.0 s, 8.75 s, 26.5 s).
  - Hard cuts to and from the black text cards.
  - A camera-shutter flash into the viewfinder (37.25 s).
  - A fast zoom through a building window into the next scene (44-46 s, 49.5 s).
- Camera: slow push-ins on scenes; maps zoom and pan (Taiwan → plane arc across the Pacific to the USA).
- Characters: small head bobs, expression swaps on key words (laugh → deadpan), arms raise a glass or a fork, the mouth talks when speaking.
