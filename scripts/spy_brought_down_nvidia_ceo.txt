[CHAPTER 1: THE JACKET]
NARRATOR: On Thursday morning, the most valuable chip company on Earth woke up without the man who started it.
NARRATOR: Thirty-three years after he sketched out his first chip in a diner booth, Jensen Huang was gone.
NARRATOR: Not fired. Not retired. Resigned, effective immediately.
NARRATOR: As president. As chief executive. As a member of the board.
NARRATOR: Every title, all at once.
NARRATOR: The filing that announced it was only a few paragraphs long.

NARRATOR: This is how one alleged spy brought down the most powerful man in artificial intelligence.
