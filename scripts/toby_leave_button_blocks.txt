TITLE: This Noob Is the Reason Roblox Has a Leave Button
FORMAT: 16:9 short story, ~57 s, one chapter. Speaker labels are for production only.

[CHAPTER 1: THE NOOB]
NARRATOR: This noob is the reason Roblox has a leave button. His name was Toby and he had one dream. Make every player feel welcome. So he joined an obby and built a giant welcome sign directly over the first jump.

TOBY: You're welcome!

NARRATOR: Nobody could see the platforms. Then he opened a free pizza stand in a racing game. Every time a car passed, Toby stopped it to ask, pepperoni or cheese? The entire race became a traffic jam. But Toby wasn't finished. He threw a surprise party in a hide and seek server. Balloons covered every hiding spot and he shouted everyone's names through a megaphone.

TOBY: Found you! Happy birthday!

NARRATOR: Players begged the moderators to remove him. One admin kicked Toby out. He rejoined with twice as many balloons. So the developers made a brand new button that let anyone escape a server instantly.

ANNOUNCER: Leave game!

NARRATOR: Players clicked it. One by one the server emptied. Finally, Toby was alone. But here's the crazy part. Toby spotted the new button and thought it was a doorbell. He clicked it 20 times and every time he rejoined he shouted, did somebody order pizza? The developers were the ones who finally logged off.
